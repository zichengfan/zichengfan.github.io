## 🏙️ Research Focus

I am a PhD candidate at the **Urban Analytics Lab**, National University of Singapore. My research sits at the fascinating intersection of **urban morphology**, **spatial perception**, and **deep learning**, with a particular emphasis on Street View Imagery (SVI) as a powerful research tool.
